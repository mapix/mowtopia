Mowtopia — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Mowtopia ("the Game") is developed and published by Weifeng Luo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Data Collection

Mowtopia does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.

2. Local Save Data

All game progress (completed levels, stars, and unlocked mowers) is stored exclusively locally on your device. No account registration or internet connection is required to play.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The Game contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and does not track users across apps or websites.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, Mowtopia is safe for players of all ages (4+).
